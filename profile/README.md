## Who we are

FOFO Studio is an independent AI design and engineering studio focused on education and the arts. We bring together technology, design, and creative practice to help institutions, brands, and creators turn ideas into useful, thoughtfully crafted products and experiences.

We care about how people learn, create, and express themselves. We see the value of technology in how it makes those activities better.

## How we work

Our approach is rooted in close collaboration. We work directly with our partners to understand the people, workflows, and practical constraints behind each project. From defining the problem and testing early ideas to design, engineering, integration, and ongoing refinement, we carry the work through to something people can use.

1. **Understand** — Define the problem and the people it serves.
2. **Explore** — Test early ideas before committing to a direction.
3. **Build** — Design and engineer the product end to end.
4. **Refine** — Integrate, learn from use, and keep improving.

## The name

> **For Others, For Ourselves.**
>
> By solving worthwhile problems for others, we build a practice worth pursuing ourselves.

FOFO is a reminder that the work has to matter on both sides: useful to the people we serve, and meaningful to the people who make it.
